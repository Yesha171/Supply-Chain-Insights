## AtlIQ Mart Supply Chain Performance Dashboard This repository contains an interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing the supply chain performance of AtlIQ Mart. The dashboard provides insights into on-time delivery, order fulfillment rates, product demand, and monthly performance trends. 
**Dashboard Overview:** The dashboard is organized into the following sections: 
1. **Supply Chain Performance Overview:** Presents high-level key performance indicators (KPIs) such as On-Time %, On-Time In-Full %, In-Full %, Line Item Fill Rate %, Value Order Fill Rate %, and total order counts. The performance across different customers and cities are shown here as well. 
2. **Monthly Performance Analysis:** Tracks monthly trends in key metrics such as On-Time %, On-Time In-Full %, In-Full %, Line Item Fill Rate %, Value Order Fill Rate %, and 'not shipped percentage'. The monthly performance helps in identifying high and low performing months. 
3. **Product Performance Analysis:** Provides insights into product-level performance, showcasing Value Order Fill Rate % and Line Item Fill Rate %. It shows which product is highly in demand and which is less, along with insights on each product category. 
4. **Product Category Performance and Delays:** This sections includes graphs showing total orders by delay periods for each product categories, along with VOFR% and LIFR% across product categories. 
5. **Summary of Supply Chain Insights:** Summarizes key findings across the entire dashboard, providing a high-level perspective on supply chain performance, and insights on each product category, and consistency across metrics. 
**Key Findings:** * Overall on-time and in-full performance is below the desired target of 86.09%. * There are significant variations in performance across customers and cities. * AM Milk products have the highest demand and faces consistent delays. * June recorded the worst performance across all metrics, with August showing significant improvement. * AM Butter is highly favored by customers, with supply issues. * AM Curd is being supplied consistently in full. * Line Item Fill Rate % and Value Order Fill Rate % shows around 1% variation across products. **Technology Used:** * Power BI **How to Use:** The dashboard provides interactive filtering capabilities. **Data Source:** The data source for this dashboard is not publicly available but represents a comprehensive set of supply chain transactions for AtlIQ Mart. **Next Steps:** The findings from this dashboard can help in: * Optimizing supply chain processes. * Addressing product specific supply chain issues. * Enhancing shipping efficiency.

Conclusion
The AtlIQ Mart supply chain is facing significant challenges in meeting its performance goals. While there are pockets of strength (VOFR and certain product categories), the overall On-Time and In-Full percentages are lagging, particularly when considering the 86.09% target. There's clear product-specific demand (AM Milk), but also delivery delay issues, and some products seem to be having challenges in the supply chain (AM Butter). June was a difficult month. However, the performance overall has been improved in August. Focusing on improving shipping efficiency, on-time delivery and supply chain management to ensure product availability will be key to improving the overall performance.
